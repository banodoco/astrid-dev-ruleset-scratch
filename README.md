# Astrid development workspace

Astrid Dev connects our creative ambition to practical contributions. Our [contribution design principles](docs/contribution-design-principles.md) keep that work rigorous and simple.

## Want to contribute? Give this to your agent.

Copy this into your agent:

```text
Help me contribute to Astrid. Start at
https://github.com/banodoco/astrid-dev and follow
skills/astrid-dev/SKILL.md. Find relevant existing
issues and project work before proposing anything new.
Help me choose the smallest useful contribution,
verify it, and follow the repository's review process.
```

Contributing without an agent? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

**Want to use Astrid, rather than develop it?** Start at **[astrid.haus](https://astrid.haus)** (recommended). For manual installation of the local Runtime and agent, follow [Set up Astrid](https://github.com/banodoco/astrid-agent/blob/main/docs/getting-started.md). You don't need to clone `astrid-app` to use the editor.

## From vision to projects

The vision defines the destination, while projects in the [plan catalog](plans/index.md) are attempts to close the gaps between our current state and that goal. Completing a plan doesn't automatically close a gap; it informs our [reassessment](docs/contribution-workflow.md#define-or-reassess-a-vision-gap).

**[Strategy](strategy.md)** maps the planned releases and their intended outcomes. Each [release brief](releases/TEMPLATE.md) is a living explanation of its highlight, intent, approach and included plans, becoming an immutable release record on publication. [Vision gaps](vision/README.md) remain outcome-based, and the [plan catalog](plans/index.md) shows every project's ownership, status, priority and estimate, taken only from its `plan.md` and [priority.md](plans/priority.md).
