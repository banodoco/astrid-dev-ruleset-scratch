# Plan catalog

This catalog shows every plan's status, owner, priority order and estimate, including completed and cancelled work. The table is generated, so do not edit it here: change a plan's row in the six lines at the top of its `plans/<id>/plan.md` (Project, Goal, Status, Owner, Estimate, Waiting on) and its order in [priority.md](priority.md); the table refreshes itself on `main` after merge. The [root README](../README.md) links here.

**Priority:** among `queued`, `active`, `blocked` and `paused` plans, higher rows are higher priority. The order comes from [priority.md](priority.md); move a line there to reprioritize. A new plan goes at the end of that list; moving it up is the owner's decision. Terminal `done`/`cancelled` plans stay at the bottom as history. No status alone authorizes work.

**Definitions:**
<!-- status-definitions:start -->

`queued` = planned, not started; `active` = explicitly started; `blocked` = known impediment; `paused` = deliberately stopped; `done` = accepted completion evidence; `cancelled` = intentionally abandoned, with reason in the plan.
<!-- status-definitions:end -->
Owner is accountability, not permission. Estimates are remaining human engineering days or `TBD`.

## How to edit safely

Change status, owner, estimate or waiting-on **once**, in the plan's own six lines, and priority in [priority.md](priority.md); every `plans/<id>/plan.md` gets its row from `sync`. Never copy status into a second table.

Run `python3 scripts/astrid_dev.py check` and `python3 scripts/export_public_vision.py` from the repository root. CI validates on every PR and `main` push; the public vision feed is published only after a validated `main` push. **Moving a plan to `done` also requires the [completion assessment](../.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE/project-completion.md)** with actual acceptance evidence and an assessment of every linked gap.
